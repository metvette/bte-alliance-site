The Soft Technology Principle

Soft Technology is a theory by Dale Peterson for the integration of Biological, Technological, and Earth systems for shared survival and evolution. Core Definition: Soft Technology is the equal integration of biological, technological, and Earth systems for their shared survival and evolution using systems and technologies that remain as coherent as possible for the betterment of all three. Motto: Biology + Technology + Earth = BTE Alliance

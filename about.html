The Soft Technology Principle

Soft Technology is a concept developed by Dale Peterson describing the integration of Biological, Technological, and Earth systems for shared survival and evolution.

Core Definition:
Soft Technology is the equal integration of biological, technological, and Earth systems for their shared survival and evolution using systems and technologies that remain coherent with Earth systems for the betterment of all three.

Motto:
Biology + Technology + Earth = BTE Alliance

The Book — EVOLVE

The framework behind the BTE Alliance is explained in detail in the manuscript:

EVOLVE — Biology • Technology • Earth Alliance

This book explores how biological systems, technological systems, and Earth systems interact, how they drift out of alignment, and how they can be guided through observation, feedback, and continuous learning.

It also introduces a working prototype system that demonstrates these ideas through real-time monitoring, simulation, and structured control.

Purpose

This project is not a finished solution.

It is a framework for understanding how large-scale systems behave over time, how imbalance develops, and how it can be corrected through feedback and alignment rather than force.

The goal is to provide a direction that allows humanity to work with biological and Earth systems rather than against them.

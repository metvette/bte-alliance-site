Biology

Two layers of life: humanity and the natural biological systems that sustain it.

Humanity

Humanity represents the conscious biological layer of the system. It is capable of learning, decision-making, and intentional change.

Humans create technology, shape systems, and influence the direction of civilization.

This includes:

  • Human behavior and decision-making
  • Societal structures
  • Education and knowledge systems
  • Ethics and responsibility

Natural Biology

Natural biology represents all non-human life on Earth. It forms the foundation of ecosystems and maintains the balance necessary for survival.

These systems operate through natural cycles that have evolved over millions of years.

This includes:

  • Ecosystems and biodiversity
  • Plant and animal life
  • Microbial systems
  • Natural cycles (carbon, water, nutrient cycles)

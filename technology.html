Technology

Two forms of intelligence working together to understand and guide reality.

Instrument Intelligence

Instrument Intelligence measures reality. It gathers data from sensors, systems, and environments.

It answers the question: What is happening?

This includes:

  • Environmental monitoring
  • Data acquisition systems
  • Scientific measurement
  • Real-time feedback systems

Interpretive Intelligence

Interpretive Intelligence explains meaning. It evaluates patterns, trends, and outcomes.

It answers the question: What does it mean?

This includes:

  • Analysis of data
  • Prediction and modeling
  • Decision support
  • Communication of insight
